# E3 run 002 — refusal threshold τ

**Hypothesis:** questions without a supporting doc get a lower top-1 cosine, so "refuse if top-1 < τ"
separates them from answerable questions.

**Config:** FiQA dev, corpus minus the 116 removed docs, exact TorchKNN 384-d. Answerable = 450 dev queries;
unanswerable = the **10 manually verified** candidates ([unanswerable set](../dataset/unanswerable.md)).
τ from 0.30 to 0.90 in steps of 0.01. `rag.refusal_curve`: answer rate = share of answerable with top-1 ≥ τ;
correct-refusal rate = share of unanswerable with top-1 < τ. No LLM calls. Script: `scripts/e3_analyze.py`.

## Results

Separation (AUC = P(top-1 of an answerable > top-1 of an unanswerable)):

| Unanswerable group | n | AUC |
|---|---|---|
| Verified (OK only) | 10 | **0.823** |
| All 50 candidates (before the manual check) | 50 | 0.558 |

Selected points of the curve:

| τ | Answer rate (n=450) | Correct-refusal rate (n=10) | Balanced (mean of both) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.50 | 0.973 | 0.10 | 0.537 |
| 0.55 | 0.913 | 0.60 | 0.757 |
| 0.58 | 0.833 | 0.70 | 0.767 |
| **0.60** | **0.760** | **0.80** | **0.780** |
| 0.62 | 0.656 | 0.80 | 0.728 |
| 0.65 | 0.511 | 0.90 | 0.706 |
| 0.70 | 0.260 | 1.00 | 0.630 |

Source: `experiments/results/e3_unanswerable/tau_curve.json`; plot `tau_curve.png` (same folder).

## Verdict: confirmed, with a strong caveat

- After the manual check the top-1 similarity does separate the groups (AUC 0.82). Before the check it
  looked useless (AUC 0.56) — because 40 of the 50 "unanswerable" candidates were in fact answerable.
  **The manual check changed the conclusion.**
- Best balanced dev threshold: **τ = 0.60** → answers 76 % of answerable questions and correctly refuses
  8 of 10 unanswerable ones. Raising τ to 0.65 refuses 9/10 but answers only 51 %.

## Caveats

- Only 10 verified unanswerable questions; τ was chosen on the same 10, so 0.80 is optimistic.
- The answerable side uses all 450 dev queries regardless of whether retrieval actually found an answer.
- Top-1 cosine depends on the encoder and on PCA; τ must be re-tuned for a 128-d config.
