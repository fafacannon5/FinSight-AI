# E2 run 004 (+ run 005) — config B vs. config A on dev

**Hypothesis (004):** combining E2's 128-d PCA with E1's IVF nprobe 16 loses at most ~5.5 points of
Recall@10 (E1 −2.82 + E2 −2.54) and gives the fastest, smallest index.
**Run 005** re-runs config A (384-d exact `torch_knn`) in the **same session** so latency is comparable.

**Config:** FiQA dev, full corpus 57,638 docs, 500 queries, k = 10, seed 42, J's machine.
004: `faiss_ivf` {nlist 240, nprobe 16}, `pca_dim` 128 (PCA fitted on the corpus only).
005: `torch_knn`, no PCA. Configs: `experiments/configs/run_e2_004.yaml`, `run_e2_005.yaml`.

## Results

| Config | Recall@10 | Δ vs A | nDCG@10 | MRR@10 | p95 (ms) | Speed-up | Memory (MB) | Build (s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A — 384-d exact (005) | 0.4665 | — | 0.3881 | 0.4532 | 2.700 | 1.0× | 84.43 | 0.0002 |
| B — 128-d + IVF 16 (004) | 0.4178 | −4.88 pt | 0.3388 | 0.3956 | 0.089 | 30.2× | 28.14 | 0.348 |

Source: `experiments/results/run_e2_004/metrics.json`, `run_e2_005/metrics.json`.

## Verdict: confirmed

- The two losses roughly add up: −4.88 pt vs. −2.82 + −2.54 = −5.36 pt expected.
- B is 30× faster at p95 and uses 3× less index memory, for about 10% relative loss of Recall@10.
- Latency is from J's machine only; do not mix with H's E1 latencies.
