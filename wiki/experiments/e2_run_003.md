# E2 run 003 — PCA 32-d

Source: `experiments/results/run_e2_003/metrics.json`.

## Hypothesis

PCA to 32-d loses more than 5 points of Recall@10 (only 45.6% of the variance is kept)

## Config

`experiments/configs/run_e2_003.yaml` — FiQA `dev`, `torch_knn` (exact), `pca_dim` 32, k 10,
`latency_queries` 200, seed 42. PCA fit on the corpus only; queries transformed with the corpus mean (Hard rule 2).
Variance kept by 32 components (corpus-only check, M7): 0.456.
